# GAIA v0.9 Non-Goals

- Do not build an autonomous coding agent.
- Do not auto-run Codex or any generated prompt.
- Do not give GAIA shell execution authority beyond existing explicit manual controls.
- Do not create or switch Git branches automatically.
- Do not commit, push, merge, or tag automatically.
- Do not modify the New Earth Dashboard repository.
- Do not modify the MicroGrow repository.
- Do not introduce unrestricted filesystem access.
- Do not move approval truth into the Dashboard.
- Do not expose signing private keys or backend internals.
- Do not weaken loopback-only assumptions without a later explicit design decision.
- Do not silently change the released v0.8 Dashboard contract.
